Mac PolCarb

Antistatic Fabric. Fabric produced in twill with antistatic properties. Weight: 109 g/m². Composition: 98% Polyester 02% Carbon. Used in the manufacture of professional clothing that requires electrostatic properties (vertical resistance and surface resistivity) in accordance with ISO 11611/2015 for welding and EN 1149-5 for antistatic protection.
