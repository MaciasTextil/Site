Our history

A 100% Brazilian company

One of the largest and most traditional textile companies dedicated to producing fabrics made of polyamide, polyester or blends of these yarns.

Founded in 1945 as Tecelagem Jacyra, the company has gone through decades of excellence and innovation in the textile industry. Its history goes back to the surname of Mr. Roberto Macias, the current Owner-Partner, who over 48 years worked across several areas — Human Resources, Financial Administration and Sales — until taking on the role of General Director.

In early 2014 a unique opportunity arose: the acquisition of all the shares held by the former partners of Tecelagem Jacyra Ltda. From that moment on, the company became Tecelagem Macias Ltda., reflecting the new phase of ownership and management.

Products and markets

Quality and versatility in every fabric

Macias Têxtil has stood out in the market by offering high-quality products and innovative textile solutions. With an unwavering commitment to excellence, the company has earned the trust and recognition of customers across different industry sectors.

Over the years, we have invested in cutting-edge technology and highly skilled teams, constantly seeking to improve our production processes. This results in highly durable, resistant and high-performance products that meet the needs and demands of today's market.

We offer a wide variety of fabrics — from the most traditional to the most technological — adapted to a range of applications and segments: apparel manufacturing, accessories, sporting goods, technical military products, and much more. Whatever the application, Tecelagem Macias stands out for the versatility and quality of its fabrics.

Our values

Tradition that looks to the future

Tecelagem Macias values sustainability at every stage of its production, adopting responsible practices, using environmentally friendly raw materials and implementing processes aimed at reducing environmental impact.

With a dedicated and committed team, Tecelagem Macias continues to follow its path of success, staying up to date with market trends and meeting constantly evolving demands. The company is proud of its journey and is ready to face the challenges and opportunities the future holds.
