Sustainability

Macias Têxtil believes that environmental responsibility is an essential part of a business committed to the future.

  • 30×Faster decomposition in our biodegradable fabrics
  • PETOcean-recycled in our line
  • BambooRenewable fiber, pesticide-free

Materials

Fabrics with lower environmental impact

Our products are developed with a focus on sustainability, using raw materials and technologies that help preserve the environment:

  • Biodegradable fabrics — They decompose up to 30 times faster than traditional fabrics, significantly reducing disposal impact.
  • Ocean-recycled PET — Fabrics made from PET bottles recovered from the ocean, helping clean the seas and reuse materials.
  • Bamboo fiber — Made from bamboo, a renewable, sustainably grown fiber with low water consumption and no need for pesticides.

Processes

Sustainable technology and processes

  • Anti-odor finish — Reduces the need for frequent washing, helping save water and energy throughout the garment's service life.
  • Optimized dyeing — A process that consumes significantly less water than conventional methods, without compromising color quality and durability.

Water

Efficient water treatment

The water used in our processes is treated in-house and returned to the system with excellent quality, which reduces the level of treatment required by sewage companies and lowers environmental impact.

Awareness

Internal commitment

Internally, we promote initiatives that encourage environmental responsibility. One example is providing reusable insulated bottles to our employees, drastically reducing the use of plastic cups and fostering a more sustainable culture within the company.
