Quality Policy

Our commitment to excellence, continuous improvement and social responsibility.

Quality policy

Our commitment

We are a textile company that produces, develops and markets woven fabric, and we are committed to providing products and services that meet our customers' needs and applicable requirements, continuously increasing their satisfaction and exceeding their expectations.

A commitment to the continuous improvement of the Quality Management System (QMS), our processes and our products, through the involvement and training of all our employees, in order to achieve the organization's objectives.

Establishing and developing partnerships and social relationships that add value, in an environment conscious of social and environmental responsibility.

Vision

To be a benchmark in the textile industry as a rising company, with an agile, organized structure focused on the development of processes and people.

Mission

To offer fabric solutions, exceeding our customers' expectations and needs, maintaining a standard of quality in products and services, in a democratic organizational environment conscious of social and environmental responsibility.

Values

We believe that by serving our customers and the market with ethics and honesty we are contributing to the development of our community (broadening our improvement), raising this awareness among our employees.
