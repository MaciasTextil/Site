Certifications

Recognized excellence with two certifications of great importance to the textile industry: ABVTEX Silver and ISO 9001.

At Macias Têxtil, we believe that excellence goes beyond the quality of our products — it is present at every stage of our operation, from the development of our fabrics to the social and environmental responsibility we embrace.

Certification

ABVTEX Silver

ABVTEX (Brazilian Textile Retail Association) is a benchmark in the sector and aims to ensure a more ethical, sustainable and transparent supply chain. The ABVTEX Silver seal recognizes our compliance with strict criteria for social, labor and environmental responsibility.

This certification ensures that we follow good practices related to:

  • Fair working conditions — We ensure that our employees work in a safe and respectful environment, free from any form of exploitation or irregular labor.
  • Sustainability in production — We adopt practices that minimize environmental impact, from the choice of raw materials to the conscious use of natural resources.
  • Ethics and transparency — We operate within the highest standards of integrity, promoting a fair and responsible value chain.

The ABVTEX Silver certification reinforces our contribution to a more sustainable market and strengthens the trust of customers and partners in our processes.

Certification

ISO 9001

ISO 9001 is one of the most recognized international standards for Quality Management, ensuring that our operations follow efficient and well-structured processes. This certification attests that we work with a quality system focused on continuous improvement and customer satisfaction.

Among the main benefits of ISO 9001, we highlight:

  • Process optimization — We implement methodologies that ensure greater efficiency and standardization across all areas of the company.
  • High-standard products — Every stage of production is rigorously controlled to ensure superior-quality fabrics.
  • Continuous improvement — We constantly monitor our processes to refine them, ensuring innovation and excellence in what we do.
