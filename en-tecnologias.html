Macias Technologies

Five proprietary technologies developed to enhance the performance, durability and comfort of our synthetic woven fabrics.

MacWear™ MacSun™ MacFlex™ MacShield™ MacFresh™

Performance and durability

MacWear™

Performance, comfort and durability for continuous use.

MacWear™ is the new performance standard for Macias fabrics. We combine superior abrasion resistance, structure retention after washing and moisture management — delivering garments that keep their appearance and comfort through intense daily use.

More than a finish, it is a technical evolution: better drape, faster drying and less wrinkling. The article leaves the factory with this technology built in.

Key benefits

  • Superior resistance to abrasion and intense use
  • Structure and appearance retention after washing
  • Moisture management with high breathability
  • Less wrinkling and fast drying

UV protection

MacSun™

UV protection built into the fabric.

MacSun™ is not a post-treatment — it is sun protection incorporated from the very construction of the fabric. It works as a functional technology for segments that demand real performance under prolonged sun exposure.

Positioned as a technical solution (not just casual fashion), MacSun™ serves brands that communicate functional protection to the end consumer.

Ideal applications

Sun-exposed workwearSchool uniformsAgriculture and constructionTechnical outdoorBrands with a functional proposition

Technical stretch

MacFlex™

Stretch and freedom of movement.

Woven fabrics with superior stretch, dimensional stability and lasting comfort, while maintaining industrial strength and durability. Proprietary combination of Polyamide 6.6 + Lycra® + MacWear.

Controlled stretch that does not deform. Superior recovery after use. Balanced thermal comfort and mechanical strength suited to intense use.

Priority applications

  • Premium corporate uniforms
  • Operational uniforms with mobility
  • Modern workwear
  • Structured casual line
  • Premium schoolwear segment

Optional finish

MacShield™

Liquid repellency and appearance protection.

MacShield™ is an optional finishing technology that adds liquid repellency to fabrics, protecting the appearance of the garment in daily use.

Technical positioning: discreet protection, not full waterproofing. Designed to reduce light stains and extend the garment's presentation throughout the day.

Priority applications

  • Corporate uniforms — presentation throughout the whole day
  • Light workwear — garments exposed to splashes and intense use
  • Functional tailoring — discreet protection without compromising elegance
  • Premium schoolwear — fewer light stains in daily use

Anti-odor

MacFresh™

Thermal comfort and a feeling of freshness.

MacFresh™ is a finish that inhibits odor-causing bacteria, keeping the fabric fresh for longer and reducing the need for frequent washing.

Technical positioning: odor reduction in prolonged use, keeping a professional appearance for longer. It works without altering the fabric's hand, drape or breathability.

Key benefits

  • Inhibits the growth of odor-causing bacteria
  • Reduces the buildup of bad odor in prolonged use
  • Keeps a feeling of freshness for longer
  • Improves the garment's hygienic performance
  • Works without altering hand, drape or breathability

Ideal applications

Workwear / Operational uniformsPremium corporate uniformsFunctional fashion / Travel wear
